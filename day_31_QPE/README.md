# Day 31 – Multi-Qubit Quantum Phase Estimation

## 🎯 Objective

Extend Quantum Phase Estimation (QPE) from a single phase qubit to a multi-qubit phase register.

The goal is to understand how additional phase qubits provide greater precision when estimating an unknown quantum phase.

---

## 🧠 Concepts Covered

- Multi-qubit Quantum Phase Estimation
- Binary representation of fractional phases
- Eigenstates
- Eigenvalues
- Controlled-U operations
- Controlled powers of U
- Phase kickback
- Inverse Quantum Fourier Transform
- Phase precision
- Measurement
